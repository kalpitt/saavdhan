Why it exists

Scam APKs spread on WhatsApp disguised as wedding invitations, courier updates, KYC notices, or electricity bills. One install sideloads banking spyware that reads OTPs and drains accounts — and resists being removed. Saavdhan finds these apps, explains what they can do, and takes you straight to the exact Settings screen to fix it. शादी के निमंत्रण या बिजली बिल के बहाने WhatsApp पर फैलने वाले स्कैम ऐप्स आपके बैंक OTP चुरा लेते हैं। सावधान उन्हें ढूँढता है और हटाने में आपकी मदद करता है।

The promises that never change

Fully offline. The app does not hold the INTERNET permission, so the operating system makes any network call impossible. Your data has nowhere to go.
Detective + guide, not enforcer. Android only lets you turn off another app's powers — so Saavdhan takes you to the right screen and coaches the final tap. It never fakes an "auto-fix".
Explainable, not magic. Every verdict lists its reasons in plain words. No black-box AI.
Calm under panic. Big buttons, simple language, one clear step at a time — in हिन्दी or English.

How it works

  1. Scan — reads public, no-root signals about each installed app: Accessibility, Device Admin, SMS access, sideloading, hidden icons, fake system names.
  2. Explain — a deterministic, fully-tested rule engine turns those signals into a risk level with reasons in plain language.
  3. Guide — a reactive checklist (isolate → strip powers → uninstall → secure accounts) that ticks itself off as you complete each step, plus a background watchdog that warns about new threats.
Honest disclaimer. Saavdhan is a defensive aid, not a guarantee. It uses behavioural heuristics (not a malware database), so it can raise false alarms and can miss brand-new threats. It guides you — it never silently changes or deletes anything. If money has already been stolen, contact your bank and cyber-crime authorities (in India: call 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in).

Install (2 minutes)

  1. Download the APK from the button above on the phone you want to protect.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If asked, allow your browser to install apps — Android will warn because the app is not from the Play Store; that warning is normal for direct downloads.
  3. Open Saavdhan, pick your language, and tap Scan my phone.
