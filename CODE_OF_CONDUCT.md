# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Saavdhan exists to protect vulnerable people from scams. We expect everyone in this community to
act with that same spirit of care. We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps the community:
- Being kind, patient, and welcoming — especially to non-technical and first-time contributors.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the people the app protects.

Unacceptable behaviour includes:
- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Using the project to build or assist anything that harms users (e.g. surveillance, malware).

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the project maintainers via a
private channel (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for private reporting, or contact the repository
owner). Maintainers will review and respond, and may remove contributions or contributors who
violate this code.
